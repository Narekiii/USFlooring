# Website Redesign Prompt for US Flooring & Molding Inc.

Create a complete modern website redesign for **US Flooring & Molding Inc.**, a local flooring store and flooring contractor located at:

**1508 W Burbank Blvd, Burbank, CA 91506**
Phone: **(818) 515-0009**
Business type: **Flooring store / flooring contractor**
Reputation: **5.0 rating with 15 Google reviews**
Main services/products: **Hardwood, laminate, vinyl, moldings, flooring materials, flooring installation, free estimates, showroom consultation**

The website should be built around the **Golden Circle framework: Why → How → What**.

The goal is to redesign the site so it does not feel like an old basic Squarespace template. It should feel like a professional, trustworthy, family-owned local flooring company that competes strongly against big-box retailers like Home Depot and Lowe’s by offering better service, better guidance, better craftsmanship, and a more personal customer experience.

The website should be conversion-focused, mobile-first, locally optimized for Burbank and surrounding Los Angeles areas, and designed to help customers call, request a free estimate, visit the showroom, or schedule an appointment.

# Design Direction

The design should feel warm, premium, practical, and local.

Visual style:

* Clean layout
* Warm neutral colors
* Natural wood tones
* Light cream/off-white backgrounds
* Dark charcoal or deep brown text
* Accent colors inspired by wood, stone, and warm interiors
* Large readable typography
* Strong mobile design
* High-quality product/showroom imagery
* Clear CTA buttons
* Rounded cards or clean editorial sections
* Trust-building layout

Suggested color palette:

* Warm Ivory: `#F5F1EA`
* Deep Charcoal: `#1E1E1C`
* Walnut Brown: `#6B4A2D`
* Sand Beige: `#D8C4A8`
* Soft Stone Gray: `#8A867D`
* Warm Gold Accent: `#C89B5A`

Avoid:

* Cold corporate blue
* Generic contractor red/black
* Overly luxury design that feels too expensive
* Cheap discount-store styling
* Cluttered product grid
* Old Squarespace template feeling
